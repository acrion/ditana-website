A short collection of recommended practices for getting the most out of Ditana. These are not exhaustive manuals — they cover the choices most likely to come up in day-to-day use, where Ditana's defaults make assumptions that are worth understanding.

## Available pages

- [**Automatic system snapshots**](/best-practices/automatic-system-snapshots/) — how snapshots are configured, and how to roll back from the rescue system if your install ever fails to boot.
- [**AUR vs Flatpak**](/best-practices/aur-vs-flatpak/) — when to reach for which, and why Ditana ships Brave from AUR rather than Flathub.
- [**Virtual machines**](/best-practices/virtual-machines/) — which hypervisors provide full 3D support for modern Wayland desktops, and which to avoid.

More pages will be added as common questions emerge. If something you'd expect to find here is missing, [open an issue](https://github.com/acrion/ditana-installer/issues) or email support@ditana.org — best-practice topics are driven by what users actually run into.
