## Stefan Zipproth

<Image src={stefan} alt="Stefan Zipproth" width={150} />

Stefan Zipproth is the lead developer and creator of Ditana. A software developer from St. Ursen, Switzerland, with a background in embedded systems and cross-platform development. He has worked in industries ranging from RF measurement and classical image processing to AI-powered medical imaging. Since 2023, he has focused on developing and refining products for the Linux ecosystem. A broader overview of his work — spanning systems architecture, high-performance C++, and knowledge representation — is available on his [personal portfolio](https://zipproth.ch).

Through **acrion innovations GmbH**, Stefan manages the financial side of Ditana while focusing solely on its development as a solo developer. This structure ensures transparent stewardship of resources for the community-driven project.

## Thomas Zipproth

<Image src={thomas} alt="Thomas Zipproth" width={150} />

Thomas Zipproth is an experienced software developer from Mindelheim, Germany, with deep expertise in image processing — particularly in document scanning and recognition technologies. He has spent his career working on high-precision systems for automated data extraction and provides valuable testing support and feedback for Ditana, helping refine its usability and reliability.
