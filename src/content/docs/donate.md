Ditana is the result of years of work, built on the conviction that a Linux distribution should explain its choices rather than hide them. It is completely free and open source, and its continued development relies on community support.

By donating, you enable me to keep enhancing Ditana — exploring new features, responding to user feedback, and maintaining the level of detail this project aims for. Every contribution makes a difference.

## Where donations go

Donations are processed through **acrion innovations GmbH**, a Swiss company I founded to manage the financial side of my projects. While the company provides the legal and financial framework, **acrion** represents my work as a solo developer. Your support directly funds Ditana's ongoing development, server infrastructure, and the time required to keep up with Arch's rolling release.

## How to support Ditana

- [**Liberapay**](https://liberapay.com/acrion/donate) — recurring donations, no platform fee.
- [**Buy Me a Coffee**](https://buymeacoffee.com/acrion) — one-off support.
- [**GitHub Sponsors**](https://github.com/sponsors/acrion) — if you already donate to other open-source work via GitHub.
- [**Patreon**](https://patreon.com/acriondev) — tiered support.

Thank you for helping keep Ditana alive, growing, and uncompromising.
